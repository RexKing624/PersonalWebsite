XerFilmLab 0.1.0

First public preview · macOS 14 or later

XerFilmLab is a native macOS workspace for converting, correcting, and exporting scanned film negatives.

Highlights

  • Color and black-and-white negative conversion with film-base sampling.
  • Non-destructive color, tone, RGB, geometry, crop, sprocket, and dust-removal tools.
  • RAW, TIFF, JPEG, PNG, and HEIC input with full-resolution export.
  • Project, preset, metadata, history, and batch workflow support.
  • Secure automatic updates through Sparkle 2 and EdDSA-signed AppCasts.
